Each wave starts at the track edge and rises away from it. Ripples are tighter where the cars are slower, so the braking zones get the most seats. Tap a wave to show its sightlines.

Bridges

Bridges run crest to crest over the track. They need at least 5 m clear above the cars.

    Prototype traced from the plan (≈ 0.75 m per plan pixel), north to the right. Cars run south to north; speeds come from a simple F1 model (3 g cornering, 4.6 g braking). Default heights scale with how hard the cars brake into each turn. Seating slope 1:3, back slope 1:2. Ripple spacing = 2.5 m + 0.12 × car speed (m/s). Sightlines start at eye level, 1.6 m above the ground, and are blocked by terrain, the crest barrier and tree canopy. Standing capacity assumes 2 people per m² on the track side of each crest; grandstands are 14 rows, 0.5 m per seat.
