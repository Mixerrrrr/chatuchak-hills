# Grand Prix: City, Circuit, Civic

Interactive prototype for an architecture studio project (INDA, Chulalongkorn University, Studio: Ken Chongsuwat).

An F1 track runs along the rail edge of Chatuchak Park, Bangkok. Four earth hills beside the turns let people watch the race:

- **Hill sliders**: change each hill's height and see how much of the track it can see (real line-of-sight, blocked by other hills and tree canopy).
- **Bridges** link the hilltops over the track and flag when they get too low above the cars.
- **Memorial** cut into Hill A faces the turn with the hardest braking.
- **Seasons**: in the rainy season, water runs down the hills into the ponds; in the dry season the ponds empty to 0%.

Everything is one file, `index.html`. No build step.

Prototype traced from the plan sketch; soil and water figures are illustrative.
